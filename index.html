01 / Biography

Beyond One Discipline.

Terrance Barksdale is a Dallas-based published author, photographer, graphic designer and creative entrepreneur whose work explores storytelling, human behavior, visual culture and personal development.

His creative interests extend across literature, photography, independent publishing, design and strategic personal branding.

Through his published works and creative projects, Barksdale continues to explore new ways of connecting ideas, people and opportunities.
